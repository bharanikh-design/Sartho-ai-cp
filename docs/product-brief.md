# Sartho product brief

## Purpose

Sartho helps an experienced technology leader find suitable roles, prove the fit with verified career evidence, prepare an honest tailored application and track outcomes.

## Primary user problem

Traditional job platforms over-index on titles and keywords. They do not understand the difference between:

- a ServiceNow developer and a ServiceNow transformation leader;
- operational desktop support and EUC transformation leadership;
- a generic project manager and an engagement leader with ITSM, M&A and digital-workplace experience.

Sartho must make those distinctions explicit.

## Agreed career lanes

1. EUC, Digital Workplace & ITSM Transformation Leadership — 40%
2. ServiceNow Engagement, Delivery & Transformation Management — 35%
3. Senior ServiceNow Business Process, Functional & Advisory — 25%

## Explicit exclusions

- ServiceNow developer-heavy roles.
- Deep platform architecture roles requiring CTA/CMA or extensive scripting.
- L3 support, desktop engineering and shift-based operations roles.
- Roles materially below the user’s current seniority.

## Core workflow

1. Build and approve Career Truth.
2. Capture a complete job description.
3. Extract mandatory, preferred and contextual requirements.
4. Map each requirement to approved evidence.
5. Explain genuine gaps and technical heaviness.
6. Recommend Apply, Review or Skip.
7. Produce a tailored application package with a complete change log.
8. Require human approval before submission.
9. Track acknowledgement, assessment, interview, rejection and offer communications.

## Non-negotiable trust rule

No generated claim may be used unless it is traceable to an approved evidence item or explicitly added and approved by the user.
