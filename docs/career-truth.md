# Career Truth policy

## Evidence states

- **Pending:** extracted from a source but not yet approved.
- **Approved:** factually correct and safe to use.
- **Rejected:** incorrect, misleading or not suitable for reuse.

## Evidence confidence

- **High:** explicitly stated in a source document with clear context.
- **Medium:** supported but requires user clarification on scope or ownership.
- **Low:** inferred and prohibited from résumé use until clarified.

## Resume safety

A résumé generator may:

- reorder approved facts;
- rewrite approved facts for clarity and relevance;
- combine closely related approved evidence;
- omit irrelevant material.

It may not:

- create new metrics;
- change job titles deceptively;
- claim hands-on engineering when the evidence is leadership or oversight;
- present planned learning as completed certification;
- turn exposure into expert-level mastery;
- use rejected or pending evidence in a submitted application.
